Reproduced locally by forcing `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Haswell` (GitHub runners dispatch a Haswell-class kernel): gemm accumulation order varies by output position across BLAS kernels (measured span 2.2e-16 under Haswell vs exactly 0 under SkylakeX), so mathematically equal entries differ in last bits and reshuffle the seeded tie order. The gate now verifies the rounding property directly (elementwise rounding maps constant rows to constant rows; equal score matrices rank identically) and records the gemm phenomenon as a measurement (`constant_gemm_score_spans`) instead of gating on it. The counterexample and noise-bound gates were already platform-stable and are unchanged.
